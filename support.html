What to include

If you are reporting a problem, please include:

  • The venue or pickup point name
  • The area or postcode, if known
  • What went wrong
  • Your phone model, if it is an app issue

Account reassurance

PickupRadar does not connect to your Uber Eats, Deliveroo, Just Eat, or other delivery platform accounts.

PickupRadar does not ask for delivery app login details, passwords, account details, customer details, or order information. Drivers share pickup reports such as wait times and venue issues.

Radar profile and email

The app creates an anonymous Radar profile automatically. No login is required to use PickupRadar or earn RadarPoints. Email sign-in is optional. It protects your RadarPoints so they can be recovered if you change or reset your phone. There is no password. The app sends a sign-in code to your email.

RadarPoints and referrals

Valid wait reports and approved pickup-point suggestions earn RadarPoints. You can invite another driver from Profile. Both of you need a protected Radar account, and they enter your code before their first valid wait report. After they complete 3 valid wait reports on at least 2 different UTC dates and at 2 different pickup points, you each earn +50 RP. Rewards are coming soon. RadarPoints cannot be redeemed yet.

Driver messages

Profile includes Messages: announcements, alerts and personal messages for your current city and Radar profile. This is not a permanent archive. Expired or withdrawn messages are not kept there.

Delete your account

You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account) or by emailing support@pickupradar.co.uk. If you did not protect your RadarPoints with an email, the app shows Delete Radar profile instead. The steps are on the Delete account page.

Missing or incorrect venues

If a pickup point is missing, duplicated, closed, or incorrectly listed, email us and we can review it.

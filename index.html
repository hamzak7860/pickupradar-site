What drivers can see and report

Live wait reports

Report a wait as under 5 minutes, 5–10, 10–15 or 15+. The current wait uses reports from the last 2 hours.

Likely fast or likely slow

With no recent wait, the app may show a clearly labelled estimate. That estimate is not a live report.

Closed or tablet issues

Recent reports can show “Reported closed recently” or “Tablet/server issue reported”.

Order starts after arrival

Note that staff start preparing when you arrive. Other drivers may see “Made when driver arrives”.

RadarPoints

Earn points for valid reports with no account. Protect them with email if you want. Rewards are coming soon and are not redeemable yet.

Missing or wrong venues

Suggest a missing pickup point, or report a wrong address, wrong branch name, duplicate or permanently closed venue.

Independent driver tool

PickupRadar is independent and is not connected to Uber Eats, Deliveroo, Just Eat, or any other delivery platform. It does not connect to delivery platform accounts and does not ask for login details, passwords, customer details, order numbers, or private order information. You can optionally say which of those apps the order was on.

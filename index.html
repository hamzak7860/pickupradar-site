    Drivers pick one of four ranges. You see the current wait and how many drivers reported it in the last 2 hours.

  • Likely fastLikely slow

    An estimate when nobody's reported

    Shown only when there's no recent wait, and always labelled, so you never mistake it for a live report.

  • Reported closed recently

    Closed or not taking orders

    Drivers can flag a pickup that's closed now or not receiving orders, before you drive there.

  • Tablet/server issue reported

    Tablet and system problems

    See when other drivers have reported the restaurant's tablet or system playing up.

  • Made when driver arrives

    Orders that start when you arrive

    Some places only start the order once a driver turns up. Drivers can say so when they report.

  • Suggest pickup point

    Missing or wrong pickup points

    Suggest a pickup point that's missing, or report a wrong address, wrong branch, duplicate or permanently closed venue.

How it works

Three steps, and the second one takes a few seconds in the car park.

  1. Open the app

    No sign-up and no password. The app creates an anonymous Radar profile on your phone.

  2. Check before you go in

    Look up the pickup and see its live wait, or a labelled estimate if nobody has reported yet.

  3. Report your wait

    Tap Report wait when you've collected. The next driver knows what to expect, and you earn points.

Independent from the delivery apps

PickupRadar is run independently. It isn't connected to Uber Eats, Deliveroo, Just Eat or any other delivery platform, and it never touches your accounts with them.

Read the privacy policy
  • No delivery app logins. It never asks for your Uber Eats, Deliveroo or Just Eat username or password.
  • No customer or order details. No names, addresses, order numbers or private order information.
  • Which app the order was on is optional. Leave it blank if you prefer.
  • You're in control. Delete your account or Radar profile from the app at any time.

Questions drivers ask

Do I need an account?

No. The app creates an anonymous Radar profile on your phone, and you can start reporting straight away. If you want to protect your RadarPoints, add your email later. There's no password: the app emails you a 6-digit code.

Where does PickupRadar work?

PickupRadar is live in Milton Keynes. London is coming next, then cities including Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds, Liverpool and Glasgow. See all the cities.

When can I download the app?

The iPhone and Android apps are coming soon. Until then, the live preview shows real Milton Keynes pickup data.

How is the current wait worked out?

From wait reports drivers made in the last 2 hours. Older reports don't set the current wait.

What do Likely fast and Likely slow mean?

They're estimates the app may show when nobody has reported a wait recently. They're always labelled, so you can tell them apart from live waits.

Does it track my location?

No background tracking. While the app is open it uses your location to show nearby pickup points, and to check you're at the pickup when you report. The privacy policy has the details.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, open Profile and tap Delete account, or Delete Radar profile if you haven't added an email. You can also email us. See Delete account for the steps.

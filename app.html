This is PickupRadar with real Milton Keynes data. Look around. Search for a pickup point and open it. Nothing you tap here is sent. You can only report in the mobile app.

  1. Pick a tab. Use Nearby to find pickups close to you when location is on. Use Fast and Slow to check waits and estimates.
  2. Search pickup points. Try a name, area or postcode, like KFC or Bletchley.
  3. Open a pickup point. The Report wait screen opens. Choose a wait time. Nothing is sent.
  • <5 minQuick
  • 5–10 minShort wait
  • 10–15 minLong wait
  • 15+ minVery long wait

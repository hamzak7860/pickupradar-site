This is PickupRadar with real Milton Keynes pickup data. Look around, search a pickup and try the report screen. Nothing you tap here is sent: reporting only works in the mobile app.

  1. Pick a tab. All, Fast and Slow show pickups by their wait or estimate.
  2. Search a pickup. Try a brand or an area, like KFC or Bletchley.
  3. Open the report screen. Choose a wait and see what drivers send from the app.
  • <5 minIn and out
  • 5–10 minA short wait
  • 10–15 minWorth knowing
  • 15+ minThink twice
